# Trademark policy

The source code is licensed under Apache-2.0 and the specifications under
CC-BY-4.0. Neither licence grants rights to the name **QUIETWIRE** or its logo.
Both are reserved.

## What you may do

- Fork the code and the protocol, modify them, and ship the result.
- Say that your product is "compatible with the QUIETWIRE protocol" or
  "based on QUIETWIRE", provided that statement is accurate.
- Refer to QUIETWIRE by name when writing about it, reviewing it or teaching it.

## What you may not do

- Ship a fork, modified build or derivative under the name QUIETWIRE, or under
  a name or logo confusingly similar to it.
- Imply that the QUIETWIRE maintainers have reviewed, audited or endorsed a
  build they did not produce.

## Why

A permissive licence invites independent implementations, which is the point.
Users must still be able to trust that a binary called QUIETWIRE came from the
audited, reproducible release process described in `SECURITY.md`. A fork ships
under its own name so that trust is never borrowed.

Questions: open a discussion at https://github.com/janpenitent/quietwire/discussions.
